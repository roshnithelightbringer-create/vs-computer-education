About Us

VS Computer Education

Located in Radheyshyam Nagar, Near Galaxy School, New Bamroli Road, Pandesara, Surat, VS Computer Education has been providing quality computer training for over 10 years. We offer a range of diploma courses including DCA, ADCA, ADCT, and PGDA , as well as specialized skill courses in Tally, Graphic Design, Hardware & Networking, and more. Our mission is to make students job-ready with practical, industry-relevant skills. We believe in personal attention, practical training, and complete placement support for every student.
