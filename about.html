About the Institute

Empowering Students with Computer Skills

VS Computer Education is a computer training institute located in Pandesara, Surat, Gujarat. We provide quality computer education to students, beginners, college students, and working professionals who want to upgrade their skills.

Our courses range from basic computer fundamentals to advanced diplomas, covering everything from Microsoft Office and Tally to graphic design, hardware networking, and textile design.

Anyone can join — no prior experience or specific educational background required.
