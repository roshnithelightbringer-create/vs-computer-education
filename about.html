About Us

VS Computer Education

Empowering students with practical computer skills since our establishment in Pandesara, Surat.

Our Mission

At VS Computer Education, we believe in empowering individuals with practical computer skills that open doors to better career opportunities. Our mission is to provide quality, affordable computer education to everyone.

Our Approach

We focus on hands-on practical training rather than just theory. Our experienced instructors guide each student personally, ensuring they gain real confidence in using computers. We keep our batch sizes small so every student gets individual attention.
